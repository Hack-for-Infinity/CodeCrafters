The Science of Accessible Reading

📢 AD: Struggling to stay focused? Try our new supplement! — (This distraction disappears in Focus Mode)

Reading is a fundamental skill that opens doors to knowledge and opportunity. For millions of people with ADHD or Dyslexia, standard web content creates unnecessary barriers. Dense paragraphs, small fonts, and cluttered layouts cause reading fatigue and make it difficult to focus on what truly matters.

NeuroRead changes this by adapting content to work with your brain rather than against it. Bionic Reading creates visual anchor points by bolding the first half of words, helping your eyes flow naturally through text and reducing cognitive load significantly.

Focus Mode strips away distractions, leaving only clean, readable content. Eye tracking follows your gaze to automatically scroll at your pace. Word Highlight guides you word-by-word, perfect for maintaining concentration when your mind wants to wander.

These aren't just features — they're tools for inclusion and cognitive accessibility. When we make the web more readable, we lower barriers to education, employment, and everyday life. Information should never feel like a chore, and with NeuroRead, it doesn't have to be.

Studies show that 15-20% of the global population has some form of reading difficulty. Yet the vast majority of digital content is designed without them in mind. NeuroRead bridges this gap by bringing powerful, science-backed tools directly to your browser — no downloads, no setup, just better reading.
